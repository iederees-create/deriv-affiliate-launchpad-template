# SEO Content Guide

The template includes:

- Route-specific title tags and meta descriptions.
- Open Graph tags.
- Canonical URLs.
- `sitemap.xml`.
- `robots.txt`.
- JSON-LD for website, blog, article, and creative work contexts.
- A blog index and long-form article.

## Suggested Blog Topics

- How broker affiliate programmes work.
- What introducing brokers should disclose.
- Trading platform comparison guides.
- Demo account education.
- Risk disclosure explainers.
- Affiliate funnel setup guides.

Write useful educational content. Do not use fake income screenshots, fake testimonials, or guaranteed-profit claims.
