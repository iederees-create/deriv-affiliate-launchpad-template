# Risk Disclosure Guide

This template includes risk and affiliate disclosure sections, but you are responsible for reviewing them before publishing.

Keep these principles:

- Do not claim guaranteed trading profits.
- Do not claim guaranteed affiliate earnings.
- Do not imply broker endorsement unless you have explicit permission.
- Disclose that the website owner may earn commissions.
- Explain that CFDs and leveraged products involve risk.
- State that the content is educational, not personal financial advice.
- Check the broker partner programme rules for your region.

If in doubt, ask the broker partner team or a qualified compliance professional to review your page before sending traffic.
