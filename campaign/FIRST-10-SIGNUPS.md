## What to measure daily

Record these numbers in a simple sheet:

- Post views
- Profile visits
- Kit-page visits
- Partner-link clicks
- Completed registrations reported in the Deriv dashboard
- WhatsApp conversations

Do not optimise for likes. The useful ratios are kit visits per profile visit, partner-link clicks per kit visit, and registrations per partner-link click.
