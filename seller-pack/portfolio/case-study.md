# Case Study

## Problem

Trading affiliates often send traffic directly to raw partner links. That gives visitors little context, hides the commercial relationship, and can feel low-trust.

## Solution

We built a rebrandable static website template that educates visitors, discloses risk, explains affiliate routing, and sends traffic through editable partner links.

## Result

The finished template gives affiliates a premium starting point with a homepage, funnel explainer, partner programme demo page, platform cards, learning hub, SEO blog, risk disclosure page, contact CTAs, buyer docs, and listing support files.

No guaranteed earnings or trading profit claims are used.
